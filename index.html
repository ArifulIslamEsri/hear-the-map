Hear the Map

Explore the world by ear. Put on headphones: left and right matter.

  • Pitch is height. Low notes are lowlands, high notes are mountains.
  • Waves are water. At or below sea level you hear a soft wash.
  • Pings are earthquakes. Closer ones echo back first; louder means stronger; brighter means shallower.
